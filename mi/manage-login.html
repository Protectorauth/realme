    Information sharing terms

    Department of Internal Affairs has requested you provide your identity information.

    What information has been requested?

    Your mobile phone number, email address and the following verified identity information:
    • full name
    • date of birth
    • place of birth
    • gender
    • verified identity expiry date.

    What purpose is my information being used for?

    RealMe needs your consent to share your information so we can:
    • process your application for a RealMe verified identity (mobile phone and email address)
    • display your verified identity information each time you access your RealMe account.

    How is my information being provided?

    RealMe and DIA's identity verification service securely share your information.

    Where is my information being sent?

    Your information is being sent to:
    • your RealMe account
    • DIA's identity verification service.

    When will it be sent?

    Every time you access your RealMe account and when your RealMe verified identity application is being processed.

    How long will it be kept?

    Your verified identity information is not retained by RealMe. Your mobile phone number and email address are not retained by DIA's identity verification service.

    Will it be used for another purpose?

    No.

    Where can I find out more?

    View the privacy statements.
